Our Story

Built by people who've worked the docks, not just the spreadsheets.

Jalix started with one broker, one warehouse, and a Rotterdam berth number. A decade later, we clear cargo through 61 countries — but we still answer the phone ourselves.

Timeline

A decade on the water.

2016

Founded in Rotterdam

Started as a two-person customs brokerage working a single berth at Rotterdam's Maasvlakte terminal.

2018

First transpacific lane

Opened ocean freight service between Shanghai and Los Angeles, our first FCL contract with a major carrier alliance.

2020

Air freight division launched

Added time-critical air cargo to handle pandemic-era medical and electronics shipments across 18 new lanes.

2022

Licensed in 40 countries

Expanded customs brokerage licensing to cover most of Asia-Pacific, the Gulf, and South America.

2025

14 regional warehouses

Opened bonded warehousing and cross-dock hubs to shorten last-mile delivery on high-volume lanes.

What We Believe
